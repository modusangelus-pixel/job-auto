# JOB-AUTO Step 2: Jobs — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The app reads the open jobs of every company it can read directly, keeps the ones that pass the owner's rule filters (region, role, level, job type), and shows them in a Jobs list with New tags, statuses and notes. The filters are editable under Settings.

**Architecture:**

- **Readers.** One small reader per job system turns that system's public job feed into plain job rows: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Personio, Workday and SuccessFactors. Every request goes through the step 1 polite fetcher, which obeys robots.txt and paces each host.
- **Store everything, filter on show.** Every job read is stored without its description: title, location, countries, job type, link and dates. The rule filters run when the list is shown, so editing a filter changes the list at once. Open and closed dates are kept for every job, for the step 4 metrics.
- **Check run.** "Check now", and an automatic check when the app opens if it hasn't checked today, read every "read directly" company, 4 at a time. The run looks like the step 1 lookup: progress, stop and a log.

**Tech Stack:** as step 1. Electron 44, React 19, TypeScript, electron-vite, better-sqlite3, vitest. No new packages.

**Spec:** `JOB-AUTO/docs/2026-11-03-job-auto-design.md`. This plan covers §11 step 2, "Jobs": §5.1–§5.3, §7.1, §7.4 (Filters only), §8.1, §8.3 and §9.2 (recording dates only).

## Decisions for the owner to check

These are calls made while planning. Facts were checked on 2026-11-04 against the owner's own data: 215 companies looked up, with 86 read directly, 111 needing Claude reading, 7 blocked and 11 not found.

1. **SmartRecruiters companies become blocked (7 companies).**
   - Why: `api.smartrecruiters.com/robots.txt` says `Disallow: /` for everyone, and SmartRecruiters' public job pages build their lists with JavaScript.
   - The design says the app never works around a block, so these companies are marked **blocked**, with the reason given.
2. **SuccessFactors is read from the company's own career site.**
   - What happens: 40 companies were marked SuccessFactors. Most run a SuccessFactors career site on their own domain, such as `jobs.scania.com` or `jobs.deere.com`. Its `/search/` pages list the jobs as plain HTML, and robots.txt allows them.
   - The problem: the old SuccessFactors servers (`career4.successfactors.com` and similar) forbid all reading in robots.txt. Step 1 often stored one of those server names as the board, and the app can't read it.
   - The fix: those companies are re-checked from their stored links, with no Claude needed. A company that only has a bare SuccessFactors server becomes **needs Claude reading**.
3. **Greenhouse EU boards are read from the board's own page.** The EU feed address doesn't exist (`boards-api.eu.greenhouse.io` doesn't resolve). The board page carries the same job list as data.
4. **Workday is read through its own job-data address.**
   - The address is `/wday/cxs/…/jobs`, the one Workday's careers site loads its jobs from. robots.txt blocks Workday's job pages, but not this address.
   - Workday shows at most 2000 jobs per search. A company over that limit is read up to 2000, and nothing is marked closed for it that day.
5. **Every job read is stored, not only the ones that fit** (changes §8.2).
   - Without descriptions this stays small, about 10 MB per 50,000 jobs.
   - It makes filter edits work at once, and keeps the full history for metrics.
6. **The experience limit (3 years) waits for step 3.** Claude reads the years, so until then every job shows as "years not stated".
7. **Checks happen two ways:** **Check now**, and automatically when the app opens if it hasn't checked today. The daily timer and start with Windows stay in step 4.
8. **No background worker process** (changes §10). Reading is network waiting, so the window stays responsive without one.
9. **Readers aren't built for Recruitee and SmartRecruiters,** since no readable company uses either. Recruitee is added when a company needs it.
10. **The 111 "needs Claude reading" companies get no jobs in this step**, as the design says. Whether to read them with Claude is decided after this step's first real check (§11, Later).

### Rules for reading sites

These are unchanged from step 1:

- obey robots.txt
- wait 1500 ms between requests to the same host
- User-Agent `JOB-AUTO/0.1 (personal job search)`
- never work around a block
- every request goes through `src/main/readers/fetcher.ts`

A request robots.txt forbids, an HTTP 403 or 429, or a challenge page makes the company **blocked**, with the reason given.

### Rule values (§5)

These are the defaults, written exactly. The owner can edit them under Settings → Filters.

**Matching rules:**

- **Folding.** Titles and words are folded before matching:
  1. `İ`, `I` and `ı` become `i`.
  2. Lower-case the text.
  3. Remove accents (NFD, then drop combining marks), so `süreç` becomes `surec`.
- **Tokens.** The folded title is split into tokens of letters and digits.
- **Words and phrases.** A word matches a whole token. A phrase matches consecutive tokens. A word ending in `*` matches any token that starts with it.

**Word lists:**

- **keep:**
  - English: `project`, `projects`, `program`, `programs`, `programme`, `programmes`, `pmo`, `quality`, `change`, `process`, `processes`, `continuous improvement`, `operational excellence`, `lean`, `delivery manager`, `coordinator`
  - Turkish: `proje`, `projesi`, `projeler*`, `kalite*`, `süreç*`, `değişim*`, `sürekli iyileştirme`, `operasyonel mükemmellik`, `yalın`, `koordinatör*`
  - Not `proje*`: it would also match Projektledare, Projektleiter and Projectleider, which §5 says must not match.
- **engineer:** `engineer`, `engineers`, `mühendis`, `mühendisi`
- **level:** `senior`, `sr`, `lead`, `principal`, `staff`, `head of`, `director`, `vp`, `vice president`, `chief`, `kıdemli`, `direktör`, `direktörü`, `müdür`, `müdürü`, `başkan*`, `lider*`
- **type:** `intern`, `interns`, `internship`, `internships`, `thesis`, `working student`, `werkstudent`, `student`, `students`, `summer`, `apprentice`, `apprenticeship`, `part time`, `praktikum`, `praktikant`, `stajyer`, `staj`, `yarı zamanlı`

**Region countries (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2):**

- **Nordics:** `SE NO FI DK`
- **Rest of Europe:** `AL AD AT BE BA BG HR CY CZ EE FR DE GR HU IS IE IT XK LV LI LT LU MT MD MC ME NL MK PL PT RO SM RS SK SI ES UA GB CH VA`
- **USA:** `US`
- **Canada:** `CA`
- **Australia:** `AU`
- **New Zealand:** `NZ`
- **Turkey:** `TR`

Every region is switched on by default.

**Filter order (`applyRules`):**

1. **type:** the employment is `intern` or `part_time`, or the title has a type word.
2. **role:** the title has no keep word.
3. **engineer:** the title has an engineer word.
4. **level:** the title has a level word.
5. **region:** the job has countries and none is in an enabled region.

A job with no countries passes and is marked location unclear.

## Review Focus

1. **A read that fails or stops partway must not close jobs.** The cases are a network error on page 7 of 20, a stop during a read, and Workday's 2000-job limit. Jobs are marked closed only by a complete read of the same board. Tests: Task 3 and Task 9.
2. **Locations come in every shape.**
   - Examples: `San Francisco, CA; New York, NY`, `Remote - US`, `Sydney, NSW`, `Atlanta, Georgia`, `İstanbul, Türkiye`, `3 Locations`, `Remote` and `London`.
   - Unknown places become location unclear and are kept. A US state code never turns into a country code (`CA`, `IN`, `DE`). Test: Task 4.
3. **Turkish letters and case.** `KIDEMLİ PROJE YÖNETİCİSİ`, `Kalite Uzmanı` and `Süreç İyileştirme Koordinatörü` must fold and match, and the owner's own words must fold the same way. Test: Task 5.
4. **A company's first read doesn't flood the list with New tags.** That covers the first read ever, the first read after a company is re-checked onto a different board, and a company added later. Test: Task 3.
5. **Huge employers.** ABB (Workday, capped at 2000) and Bosch-sized SuccessFactors sites must page, stay inside the per-host pace, and be stoppable mid-company. A stop keeps the other companies' finished reads. Tests: Task 7, Task 8 and Task 9.

**Rules in `saveRead`:**

- It runs as one transaction and sets `read_at`, `read_board` and `read_error = NULL`.
- **Baseline:** a job gets `baseline = 1` when the company's `read_board` before this read differs from `board` (null counts as different).
- **Updating a stored job:** title, location, url, `last_seen` and `closed_at = NULL` are updated. Countries, employment and `posted_at` are kept when the stored row has `details_read = 1` and the incoming job still `needsDetails`.
- **`details_read`** is set to 1 when a job arrives with `needsDetails = false` from a reader that sets it, which is Workday after details.
- **Closing:** only when `complete` and the board is unchanged, open jobs of the company that this read didn't contain get `closed_at = now`.
- **New:** `isNew` = not baseline, no status, and `first_seen` within 7 × 24 h of `now`.

### Task 4: Locations and region groups

**Files:**
- Create: `src/main/jobs/location.ts`
- Test: `tests/jobs/location.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `countriesIn(text: string): string[]`: unique alpha-2 codes, in the order found.
  - `countryCode(name: string): string | null`: a country name or alias, or an alpha-2 code passed as is, mapped to its code.
  - `REGION_COUNTRIES: Record<RegionGroup, string[]>`: exactly the Global Constraints lists.
  - `regionGroupsOf(countries: string[]): RegionGroup[]`

**Rules:**

- **Splitting:** the text is split into places on `;`, `|` and ` / `. Each place is split into parts on `,`, ` - `, `(` and `)`. Parts are read from last to first, and the first one that resolves gives that place's country:
  1. **Country names:** English names come from `new Intl.DisplayNames(['en'], { type: 'region' })` for every code in a fixed list of all alpha-2 codes. Aliases: `USA`, `US`, `U.S.`, `United States of America`, `UK`, `U.K.`, `England`, `Scotland`, `Wales`, `Northern Ireland`, `Great Britain`, `Türkiye`, `Turkiye`, `Sverige`, `Norge`, `Suomi`, `Danmark`, `Deutschland`, `Österreich`, `Schweiz`, `Suisse`, `Svizzera`, `España`, `Italia`, `Nederland`, `Polska`, `Česko`, `Czechia`.
  2. **US states:** the 50 states and DC, by name or 2-letter code. `Georgia` is the US state.
  3. **Canadian provinces and territories:** by name or code (`AB BC MB NB NL NS NT NU ON PE QC SK YT`).
  4. **Australian states:** by name, or by code `NSW VIC QLD TAS ACT`.
- **No bare codes:** other 2-letter parts are never read as country codes. `countryCode` is the one function that accepts a bare alpha-2 code.
- **Folding:** matching folds case and accents, as in the rule values.

### Task 9: Job check run

**Files:**
- Create: `src/main/jobs/check.ts`
- Test: `tests/jobs/check.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `READERS`, `saveRead`, `noteReadFailed`, `knownDetails`, `titleFits`, `loadFilters`, `regionGroupsOf` and `fittingJobs`.
- Produces in `src/shared/types.ts`:
  - `JobCheckProgress { running: boolean; done: number; total: number; current: string[]; lastSummary: string | null; lastCheckAt: string | null }`
- Produces in `src/main/jobs/check.ts`:
  - `createJobCheck(deps: { db: Db; fetcher: Fetcher; log(line: string): void; onProgress(p: JobCheckProgress): void; readers?: Partial<Record<JobSystem, Reader>>; now?: () => Date; parallel?: number }): JobCheck`
  - `JobCheck { start(): Promise<void>; stop(): void; progress(): JobCheckProgress }`. `start` never rejects, and a start while running joins the run.
  - `shouldCheckOnOpen(lastCheckAt: string | null, now: Date): boolean`. It is true when `lastCheckAt` is null or falls on an earlier local date.

**Rules:**

- **Which companies:** every company with status `direct`, read `parallel` at a time (default 4).
- **Each company:**
  1. `list(fetcher, board, now())`.
  2. Merge `knownDetails` into the rows (`needsDetails` becomes false where known).
  3. For each row still needing details, call `details` when `titleFits(title)` holds and either the row has no countries or `regionGroupsOf(countries)` meets an enabled region.
  4. Call `saveRead(db, id, `${system}:${board}`, rows, complete, now)`.
- **Errors:**
  - A `BlockedError` → `noteReadFailed(..., blocked = true)`.
  - Any other error → `noteReadFailed(..., blocked = false)`.
  - No reader for the system → `noteReadFailed(db, id, 'No job reader for <system> yet', false, now)`.
- **Stop:** the signal is checked between pages and between detail reads. A company cut short by stop saves nothing.
- **Logs:**
  - `job check started: <n> companies`
  - `read <name>: <n> jobs (<added> new, <closed> closed)`, with ` — <note>` appended when there is a note
  - `read <name> failed: <error>`
  - `job check finished`
  - `job check stopped`
- **Summary:** `Checked <n> companies: <open> open jobs, <fit> fit your filters.` Add ` <k> could not be read.` when k > 0. On a stop, the summary is `Stopped.`
- **lastCheckAt:** settings key `lastCheckAt` is set to `now` when the run finishes, but not when it's stopped. Progress carries `lastCheckAt` from settings at all times.

  - `a blocked read marks the company blocked; another error only records it; both leave jobs open`
  - `a company whose system has no reader records that, and the run goes on`
  - `details are read only for rows whose titles fit and whose region is on or unknown, and only once`
  - `up to 4 companies are read at once`
  - `stop mid-company saves nothing for it, keeps finished companies, and does not set lastCheckAt`
  - `summary counts open and fitting jobs, and failures`
  - `shouldCheckOnOpen: null → true, earlier today → false, yesterday 23:59 → true`
